### The big idea

Now with the help with AI, digital word speed is faster and faster.
But I want to know how AI improve productivity in real world.
Take chips as an example.
An AI chip does one thing very well: lots of math at the same time.
Making one takes five stages, and each stage is led by a famous company.

### Design — NVIDIA

Engineers decide what the chip does and how it is built.
They write the design as code.
NVIDIA is the most famous AI chip designer. It doesn't make chips. It only designs them.

To do this, designers use special software called EDA tools.
Synopsys is the biggest company making them.

### Manufacture — TSMC

A factory prints the chip on silicon wafers.
TSMC makes almost all of the world's top AI chips, for NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, and Google.

To print the newest chips, TSMC uses EUV machines.
Only ASML makes them.

### Memory — SK hynix

AI chips need very fast memory called HBM, placed right next to the chip.
SK hynix is the leader in HBM.
The memory is made in its own factories at the same time, then sent to packaging.

### Package — TSMC

The chip and the memory are put together into one package.
TSMC's CoWoS packaging is used for most top AI chips.

### Software — NVIDIA

A chip is useless without software to run AI models on it.
The software is written while the chip is being designed, so it is ready when the chip ships.
NVIDIA's CUDA is the most famous, and it is a big reason NVIDIA wins.
