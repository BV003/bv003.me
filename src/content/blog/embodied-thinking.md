### Discrete Research directions

Just like LLM, this area is very impetuous. Some people do some very shallow research and then rush to publish.

Not like LLM, the research in physical AI doesn't
have a clear direction. VLM, VAM, WAM. People are in research stage and don't
find ChatGPT in physical AI yet.


### Use Specialized Model
We used to use a specific model named VAM or WAM to control robots. Such models
have unique structures and are trained in small sizes, like billions of parameters.
So in some areas they don't perform well.


### Use VLM Directly
And recently general VLMs have started controlling robot parts directly, which
means we got rid of VAM. OpenAI and Anthropic all show physical ability in their most frontier models.
The advantage is that the parameters are very big and you don't need to train new models.
Maybe we still need to overcome something in speed and other details.
Maybe this is the right way.

### Auto Drive

I think auto drive is a kind of Physical AI. 
And in 2026, it seems to work pretty well.
I have tried Tesla and its autodrive is impressive.
So this is a sign that Physical AI will work out. 

### In Conclusion 
I think it needs 5 years to have some demo-level applications.
And that's a good time to work in the Physical AI industry at that time.
