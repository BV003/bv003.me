### Discrete Rearch directions

Just like LLM, this area is very impetuous. People do some very swallow research and then publish ahead of schedule.

Not like LLM, the research in phycial AI don't 
have a clear direction. VLM, VAM, WAM. People are in research stage and don't
find ChatGPT in phycial AI yet.

### System 1 and System 2

#### System1
We used to use specifical model named VAM or WAM to control robot. Such models 
have unique sturtures and trained in small sizes like Billion paramaters.
So in some areas it don't perform well.


#### System2
And recently there is one thing is that general VLM can control robot part directly.
Which mean we get rid of VAM. OpenAI and Antropic all show Physical ability in their most front model.
The advantage is that the paramaters are very big and you don't need to train new models
Maybe we still need to overcome something in speed and other details.
Maybe this is the right way.
