### Discrete Research directions

Just like LLM, this area is very impetuous. Some people do some very shallow research and then publish ahead of schedule.

Not like LLM, the research in physical AI doesn't
have a clear direction. VLM, VAM, WAM. People are in research stage and don't
find ChatGPT in physical AI yet.


### Use Specialized Model
We used to use specific model named VAM or WAM to control robot. Such models
have unique structures and trained in small sizes like Billion parameters.
So in some areas it don't perform well.


### Use VLM Directlly
And recently there is one thing is that general VLM can control robot part directly.
Which mean we get rid of VAM. OpenAI and Anthropic all show Physical ability in their most frontier model.
The advantage is that the parameters are very big and you don't need to train new models
Maybe we still need to overcome something in speed and other details.
Maybe this is the right way.

### Auto Drive

I think auto drive is a kind of Physical AI. 
And in 2026, it seems work pretty well.
I have try Tesla and its autodrive is impressive.
So this is a sign that Physical AI will work out. 

### In Conclusion 
I think it need 5 years to have some demo level application.
And that's a good time to working in Physical AI industry at that time.
