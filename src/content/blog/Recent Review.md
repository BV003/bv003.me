### Jan 2026 - Apr 2026

#### What I am working on

Since January 2026, I have been interning at a company. It is quite busy, and I spend most of my daytime on it. In the meantime, I am waiting for my U.S. master’s offers and finishing my graduation project.

#### Too busy

I think some days are very tough. The main reason is that I need to do my internship and my graduation project at the same time. On some days, I have to spend my evenings and weekends working on my graduation project after work, which leaves me with almost no rest time. I often feel quite overwhelmed during those periods. Without rest, it becomes difficult to produce high-quality work, plan for the future, or explore new opportunities.

#### Physical Health

Another area I haven’t managed well is recovering from my rotator cuff injury. It has been an ongoing issue since last summer, but I initially underestimated its severity. As a result, I had to stop going to the gym. It wasn’t until last month that I fully recognized the seriousness of the injury and made a conscious decision to prioritize recovery.

#### Too many short videos

I’ve realized that I spend too much time on short video platforms like TikTok and Tencent. Even though I’m aware of how addictive they can be, I still find myself falling into the same pattern. This has negatively affected my focus and productivity. Moving forward, I want to take more control and gradually reduce my reliance on these platforms.
