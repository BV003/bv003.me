Welcome to my blog! This is where I'll be sharing my thoughts on artificial intelligence research, software engineering, and various topics that interest me.

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

- **AI Research**: Insights from my work on large language models, multi-agent systems, and interpretability
- **Engineering**: Best practices, lessons learned, and interesting technical challenges
- **Investing & Web3**: Thoughts on technology trends and the future of finance
- **Personal Reflections**: On learning, growth, and the pursuit of excellence

## Why I Started This Blog

Writing is thinking. By articulating ideas clearly, I can better understand them myself. This blog serves as both a public notebook and a way to connect with like-minded individuals.

I believe in the power of open knowledge sharing. The AI community has benefited immensely from researchers and engineers who openly share their work, and I want to contribute to this culture.

## Get in Touch

If you find anything interesting here or want to discuss ideas, feel free to reach out via email or GitHub. I'm always open to collaborations and conversations.

Thanks for reading!
