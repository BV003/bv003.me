The goal of becoming one of the top engineers in the world is ambitious, but I believe it's achievable with the right mindset and approach. Here are my thoughts on what this journey entails.

## Technical Depth and Breadth

Top engineers possess both deep expertise in specific domains and a broad understanding of adjacent fields. This T-shaped knowledge allows them to:

- Solve complex problems in their area of expertise
- Collaborate effectively with specialists in other domains
- Make architectural decisions that consider the full system

## Continuous Learning

Technology evolves rapidly. What separates good engineers from great ones is the commitment to continuous learning. This means:

- Reading research papers and staying current with developments
- Building side projects to explore new technologies
- Learning from failures and successes alike

## Building Things That Matter

Ultimately, engineering is about creating value. The best engineers work on problems that matter, whether that's advancing AI capabilities, improving infrastructure, or building products that help people.

## The Long Game

Becoming a top engineer is a marathon, not a sprint. It requires patience, persistence, and a willingness to do the hard work that others avoid.

This is the path I'm committed to walking.
